Amazon AWD shipment upload format

The FBA or AWD box label PDF Amazon gives you. Each label's SKU is read off the label; our template (need label · name · UPC · spot) is printed in an empty spot on it.

✅ Already packed (PREVIOUS PACKED) — ASIN or SKU, one per line
Upload the labels after this: their labels say ✅ PREVIOUS PACKED — already on the pallet instead of our template (no trip to the warehouse). A number after it = only that many boxes.

📭 Not Received

Items we shipped to FBA or AWD where everything else in the shipment was received (or Amazon closed it) but this item is still short 10+ days later. They are no longer counted as stock in FBA / AWD, so the recommendations ask you to ship more. Tap what happened.
