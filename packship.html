Today's Manifest
Adds any new shipped orders from Veeqo to today's manifest. Already-scanned orders are not duplicated. This runs automatically every hour — use this button to force an immediate refresh.
Tracking Monitor
Checks two things: labels not scanned by carrier within 2 days of shipping, and packages not delivered after 10 business days. Reads directly from your Scan Log — no mass order fetching. Runs automatically at 8am & 4pm EST. For a full 30-day audit, use the Status Check tab with a Veeqo CSV export.
Backfill Real Weight
Fills in the real Veeqo shipment weight for a day's orders that don't have it yet (scanned/picked before this feature existed). Rescanning won't do this — a duplicate scan is rejected before it ever checks Veeqo — so use this button instead. Processes up to 50 at a time; click again if it says more are left. Defaults to today, but pick an earlier date if that's where the orders you're checking actually landed.
⚠ Weight still looks wrong after backfilling?
That order already has SOME number saved — the button above skips it no matter how many times you run it. Use Force below to overwrite it with a fresh, correctly-converted number straight from Veeqo.
Runs automatically in batches until every order's weight has been re-checked against Veeqo — can take a while for a lot of history. Safe to stop and restart later.
