💲 Change Amazon prices. One row per ASIN: a new price goes to every SKU of that ASIN (FBA + FBM). Tap a 💡 suggested price (or 💡 Fill all) — the business price follows at $0.03 less — check the row turns ✅, then Change on Amazon. Amazon shows the new price in about 5–15 minutes. Every change is kept (who, when, old → new, Amazon's answer) in 🕘 History below.
📜 Amazon's rules this checks
  • Maximum price (only if the listing has one): a price above it makes the listing inactive — the app won't send it.
  • Minimum price (not required by Amazon; only if the listing has one): a price below it makes the listing inactive — the app won't send it.
  • Business price must be lower than the price; every quantity-discount tier must be lower again — the app won't send it otherwise.
  • Big jumps: Amazon's Fair Pricing Policy takes down offers it sees as a "potential pricing error" (priced well above recent prices). Amazon does not publish a %; raising more than 30% at once needs a second OK here — smaller steps are safer.
  • Was price = what buyers paid on Amazon over the last 90 days (typical). Competitive Price Threshold = other stores' price: above it the listing can lose the Buy Box (it stays up). Tap 🔎 to get them.
